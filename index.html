🌌 Telegram graph visualization

The evolution of Scandinavian telegram channels over time.
Nodes represent channels, links represent messages forwarded between the channels; these links decay with a time constant of τ ≈ 1 day. The visualization shows clustering along the different languages, together with emergent cross-links. Hover over a node to see the channel name and to explore the embedding of the node in the network.

  • 391 nodes (channels)
  • 1890 distinct links
  • 57933 forwarded messages
